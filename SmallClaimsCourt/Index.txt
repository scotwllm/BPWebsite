Los Angeles Small Claims Court resolves disputes under $12,500
 for individuals ($6250
 for businesses) through an informal, lawyer-free process. Cases are filed with the Superior Court of Los Angeles County and must be filed where the defendant lives, works, or where the damage occurred. Filing fees range from $30 to $75.
https://www.lacourt.ca.gov/pages/lp/small-claims

Attorney Representation: Lawyers are not allowed to represent parties during the initial hearing, but you may consult with one beforehand.
Where to File: Cases are filed at the Superior Court of Los Angeles County.
Filing Fees: Fees depend on the amount claimed: 
 $30 (up to $1,500)
$50 ($1500 up to $5000
$75 ($5000 up to $12,500)

Procedure: You can file in person or by mail with the County of Los Angeles.
Assistance: The Los Angeles County Department of Consumer and Business Affairs (DCBA) provides free advisors to assist with your case. 

Common Reasons to File
Unpaid debts or loans
Landlord-tenant disputes
Property damage
Auto accidents
Contract disputes 

Before Filing
You must ask the defendant to pay or fix the issue before filing a lawsuit. If they refuse, you can file the Plaintiff’s Claim and ORDER to Go to Small Claims Court (Form SC-100).
