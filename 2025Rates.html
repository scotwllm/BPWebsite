2025 Editing Rates

Clear, Fair Pricing for Every Level of Editing

December 2025

Quality editing shouldn’t require detective work to figure out the price.

For 2025, I’ve structured my rates to be simple, transparent, and aligned with industry norms—but without the inflated agency markup. Everything below reflects the work involved, the time required, and the experience I bring to each project.

Whether you need a quick proofread or a deep structural evaluation, you’ll know exactly what you're paying for, with no surprises.

✦ Proofreading—$0.014 per word

The final polish before your document goes out into the world. This includes:

  • typos
  • double words
  • punctuation errors
  • minor formatting inconsistencies

Proofreading assumes the document is already well-structured and well-written. If sentences need rewriting, that falls under copyediting or line editing.

✦ Copyediting—$0.025 per word

A deeper pass that improves clarity, correctness, and consistency. This includes:

  • grammar and usage
  • sentence-level corrections
  • word choice improvements
  • consistency across style and terminology
  • fixing "echo words"

✦ Line Editing—$0.045 per word

The artistry layer. Line editing refines the voice, tone, rhythm, and flow. This includes:

  • sentence restructuring
  • voice and phrasing
  • dialogue polishing (for fiction)
  • coherence and concision

If you want your writing to *sing*, this is the level that does it.

✦ Developmental Editing—$0.09 per word

Big-picture structure for manuscripts, reports, long-form content, or anything with multiple chapters or sections. This includes:

  • organization
  • logic and pacing
  • argumentation (for nonfiction)
  • plot, characterization, and scene order (for fiction)

Developmental editing comes first—before any proofreading or lower-level edits.

✦ Photo Editing—$28 per hour

Basic adjustments, blemish cleanup, color fixes, simple composites, and preparing images for web or print.

✦ Portrait Retouching—$42 per hour

Magazine-style retouching with natural, realistic results. No plastic skin, no uncanny valley.

Why publish rates publicly?

Because transparency builds trust. You shouldn’t have to email five editors just to learn whether you can afford basic proofreading. And as a former corporate communications manager, my pricing reflects both industry norms and the value of delivering polished, professional work the first time.
